1. About Neon

Neon is an open-source Discord bot designed to help communities run Capture The Flag (CTF) challenges, manage submissions, record winners, and display leaderboards.

I am the independent developer of Neon. I provide the bot's source code for others to inspect, modify, and self-host. I do not operate a publicly hosted bot service as part of this project.

2. Using Neon

By using Neon in a Discord server, you agree to use it responsibly and follow Discord's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, and applicable laws.

You must not use Neon to facilitate illegal activities, harass other users, abuse Discord, or disrupt other communities.

Server administrators are responsible for configuring and operating their own Neon instances appropriately.

3. CTF Challenges and Submissions

Neon supports challenge creation, flag submissions, winner recording, scoring, and leaderboards.

Notice on Public Flag Submissions: Depending on the server's configuration, flag submissions may be posted in a public Discord channel. By submitting a flag, you understand that the submitted content may be visible to other members who can access that channel.

Challenge authors or authorized administrators are responsible for manually reviewing submissions and determining winners. Neon does not automatically verify whether a submitted flag is correct.

Points and rankings depend on the challenge configuration and the actions of authorized administrators. Users should not attempt to manipulate submissions, timestamps, scoring, or winner records.
